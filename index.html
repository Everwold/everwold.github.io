Pick up a weapon.
Become a class.

Questions

Before you ask.

When can I play?

Not yet. EVERWOLD is in active development. Playtests are announced first in the Discord.

Is it really an MMORPG?

That's what we are building. Today the island is played alone; the shared world, group dungeons and trading between players are the next big steps. This page marks everything as live or coming.

Will there be classes?

Yes, and you won't pick them from a menu. Your weapon becomes your class: six paths across sword, spear and bow come first, magic after.

Is the footage real?

Yes. Every picture and clip here is recorded from the current build.

How can I shape the game?

Join the Discord. Every week there's a new update, and the community votes on what comes next.
